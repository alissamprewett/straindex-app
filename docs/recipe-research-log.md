# StrainDex Recipe Research Log

Purpose: track what's been researched, which sources were useful vs. dead
ends, and what's queued up for the next batch — so future work can pick up
where this left off instead of re-covering the same ground.

**Important note on approach:** there is no legitimate bulk "open source
cannabis recipes" dataset (checked — see Dead Ends below). All recipes in
this app are written from scratch, informed by real, verified cooking
technique research, not copied from any single source. This keeps things
copyright-clean and lets us verify technique details (temperatures, times,
safety notes) independently rather than trusting one site's numbers.

## Batch 1 — Original launch content (6 recipes)
Classic Cannabutter, Canna-Infused Olive Oil, DIY Cannabis Tincture, and 3

## Batch 2 — Category fill to 10-each (41 recipes)
Filled all 6 categories (Infusion Base, Baked Goods, Gummies & Candy,
Drinks, Topicals, Savory & Snacks) to 10 recipes each. General technique
verification via web search (decarb temps/times, standard dosing ranges).

## Batch 3 — Infusion Base deep dive (8 recipes)
Added: Sous Vide Cannabutter, Slow Cooker Cannabutter, Glycerin Tincture,
Infused Milk, Infused Sugar, Infused Salt, Infused Flour, RSO.

**Sources checked this batch:**
- Sous vide cannabutter technique — cross-verified across 5 independent
  sources (Rollitup forum, MiCannaTrail, Anova's own recipe database,
  Medible Review): consistent numbers were decarb at 200–203°F for
  1.5–2 hrs, then infuse at 185°F for 4 hrs.
- RSO safety specifics — cross-verified across 6 sources (Cannabis
  Training University, High There, Leafly, Main Street Health, GB The
  Green Brand, Birch & Fog): universal agreement on ventilation, no open
  flame, electric heat only, ethanol flash point ~62°F. Dosing guidance
  (rice-grain-sized starting dose) also cross-verified.

**Dead ends / sources deliberately not used:**
- No dedicated "open source cannabis recipes" repository or dataset
  exists (searched directly — results were about open-source *cannabis
  data/genetics* projects like OpenTHC and Open Cannabis Project, not
  recipes).
- GrowWeedEasy reader photo/recipe galleries — reader-submitted content
  on a private site, not under an open license; skipped for the same
  reason Leafly/SeedFinder strain data was skipped earlier.
